# Onwan AI Transparency Notice / KI-Transparenzhinweis / إشعار شفافية الذكاء الاصطناعي

> **Draft updated 2026-07-27.** This notice defines launch behavior that must be verified in staging
> before publication. It is not a substitute for the Privacy Policy or editorial/legal review.

## English

### You are using an AI system

Onwan is an AI-assisted journalism workflow. When a user runs an Onwan action, document text selected
for that action is processed by a managed Google Gemini service. The interface must clearly identify
AI actions and must not pretend that an output was written or verified by a human.

### What AI does

Depending on the selected action, AI may propose story ideas, prepare a proposal, organize research,
build a fieldwork plan, suggest a story structure, prepare a review copy of a draft, or identify claims
that require verification.

### What AI does not do

Onwan does not become the reporter, source, fact-checker, lawyer, safety adviser, or accountable
editor. Output may contain invented facts, false citations, missing context, bias, or unsafe advice.
The journalist must verify facts, quotations, links, names, dates, rights, and risks before use.

### Human editorial control

The user decides what to accept, edit, reject, or publish. Onwan does not automatically publish
content. Before Google makes programmatic suggestion/comment writing generally available, Onwan
preserves the original draft and creates a clearly labelled review copy with short reasons.

### Publication and labelling

From 2 August 2026, Article 50 of the EU AI Act introduces transparency duties for certain AI
systems and AI-generated or manipulated content. Text published to inform the public on matters of
public interest can require AI-origin disclosure unless it has undergone human review or editorial
control and a natural or legal person holds editorial responsibility. Users must apply the rule to
their publication context and any stricter newsroom policy. Onwan's interface and exported metadata
must make AI involvement identifiable; a journalist must not remove a legally required disclosure.

### Models and data

The managed launch uses Google Gemini through Google Cloud. The former owner-only Anthropic runtime
path has been removed from the Phase 1 source; staging verification remains pending. Onwan does not
intentionally use customer editorial text to train its own models. Final Google Cloud data-use
settings and contracts must be verified before this statement is published.

## Deutsch

Onwan ist ein KI-gestütztes journalistisches Arbeitswerkzeug. Die Oberfläche muss deutlich machen,
wann ein Arbeitsschritt von KI unterstützt wird. KI-Ausgaben können erfundene Fakten, falsche Quellen,
fehlenden Kontext oder Verzerrungen enthalten. Der Mensch bleibt für Recherche, Verifikation,
Quellenschutz, Rechteklärung, Sicherheit und Veröffentlichung verantwortlich.

Ab dem 02.08.2026 gelten Transparenzpflichten nach Art. 50 der EU-KI-Verordnung. Bei KI-generierten
Texten zu Angelegenheiten von öffentlichem Interesse kann eine Kennzeichnungspflicht bestehen, wenn
keine menschliche Prüfung oder redaktionelle Kontrolle mit klarer redaktioneller Verantwortung
stattgefunden hat. Onwan veröffentlicht niemals automatisch.

Kontakt: [support@onwan.eu](mailto:support@onwan.eu)

الاصطناعي. قد تحتوي النتائج على معلومات مختلقة أو مصادر خاطئة أو سياق ناقص أو تحيز. يبقى الإنسان

الإفصاح عن أصل النص المدعوم بالذكاء الاصطناعي المنشور لإخبار الجمهور بشأن مسألة ذات مصلحة عامة إذا
لم يخضع لمراجعة بشرية أو رقابة تحريرية مع مسؤولية واضحة. لا ينشر عنوان المحتوى تلقائياً.
