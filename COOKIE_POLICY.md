# Onwan Cookie Policy / Cookie-Hinweise / سياسة ملفات الارتباط

> **Draft dated 2026-07-26.** Verify this document against the deployed website and add-on before
> publication. Do not list fictional cookie names.

## English

### Current launch design

Onwan's planned public information pages do not use advertising, social-media, or behavioural
tracking cookies. The Google Docs add-on stores necessary settings such as interface language and
licence state through Google Apps Script/User Properties rather than Onwan advertising cookies.

The hosting platform necessarily receives technical connection information such as an IP address to
deliver and secure the service. The final deployment must be audited to confirm which cookies or local
storage entries are actually created and their retention periods.

### Third-party pages

When a user opens Google Workspace, Google Cloud-hosted functionality, or Stripe Checkout, those
providers can use their own strictly necessary security, authentication, fraud-prevention, and payment
technologies under their policies. Onwan does not control cookies placed on a provider's separate
domain.

### Future analytics

Onwan will not add optional analytics, marketing, or advertising cookies without first updating this
policy and, where legally required, obtaining consent before placing them. Rejecting optional cookies
must not block core paid functionality.

Questions: [support@onwan.eu](mailto:support@onwan.eu)

## Deutsch

Die geplanten öffentlichen Onwan-Seiten verwenden keine Werbe-, Social-Media- oder
Tracking-Cookies. Notwendige Einstellungen der Google-Docs-Erweiterung werden über Funktionen von
Google Apps Script gespeichert. Google und Stripe können auf ihren eigenen Seiten technisch
notwendige Sicherheits-, Anmelde-, Betrugspräventions- und Zahlungsmechanismen einsetzen.

Vor dem Start wird die tatsächlich ausgelieferte Website technisch geprüft. Optionale Analyse- oder
Marketing-Cookies werden nur nach Aktualisierung dieser Hinweise und – soweit erforderlich – nach
vorheriger Einwilligung eingesetzt.

Kontakt: [support@onwan.eu](mailto:support@onwan.eu)

## العربية

إعدادات إضافة مستندات Google الضرورية عبر خدمات Google Apps Script. وقد تستخدم Google وStripe في
صفحاتها المنفصلة تقنيات ضرورية للأمان وتسجيل الدخول ومنع الاحتيال والدفع.

يجب فحص النسخة المنشورة تقنياً قبل الإطلاق. لن نضيف ملفات تحليل أو تسويق اختيارية من دون تحديث هذه
السياسة والحصول على الموافقة المسبقة عندما يفرض القانون ذلك.
